01

Site name

Naija Field Guide

“Naija” is a familiar nickname for Nigeria, while “Field Guide” suggests a friendly, practical reference. The name feels welcoming to both local readers and people discovering the country for the first time.

Possible domain: naijafieldguide.org (availability not checked)

N Naija Field Guide
02

Site purpose

Naija Field Guide will introduce Nigeria through concise, reliable information about its geography, people, culture, and travel highlights. Visitors will be able to explore key facts, learn about regional traditions and places, and send a question or suggestion through a simple contact form.

  • Home: Country overview, quick facts, and a featured place.
  • Culture: Languages, food, music, and traditions from Nigeria's diverse communities.
  • Explore: Places to visit, practical travel notes, and a visitor question form.
03

Visitor scenarios

These questions represent the information a first-time visitor or curious learner may be looking for:

  • What should I know about Nigeria's regions, languages, and currency before I visit?
  • Which Nigerian places and cultural experiences can I explore?
  • How can I ask a question or suggest a topic for the guide?
