From the field

Traditions keep moving.

Across Nigeria, heritage is not only preserved in museums. It is carried in family recipes, spoken languages, music, festivals, and art in public places.

The sculptures at Tinubu Square offer a glimpse of Yoruba spiritual figures in the middle of modern Lagos: a reminder that old stories continue to share space with a fast-changing city.
